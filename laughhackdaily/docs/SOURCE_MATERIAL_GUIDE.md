# Source Material Reference Guide

## Translation Principles

**Golden Rule:** Transform every concept into a modern behavioral observation using second-person framing ("you" / "people").

**Never:** Quote directly, name authors, reference law numbers/chapters, use academic language.

## Content Idea Bank

### High-Performing Hook Patterns

1. "The reason you feel [emotion] is because..."
2. "Notice how [observation] always happens..."
3. "People who [trait] tend to..."
4. "The moment you [action], you lose..."
5. "There's a reason [phenomenon] works..."
6. "Watch how [behavior] reveals..."
7. "If you keep [pattern], it's because..."
8. "The fastest way to [result] is..."
9. "People don't realize that [insight]..."
10. "Be careful when someone [behavior]..."

### Universal Themes That Perform

- Silence and power
- Emotional control
- Social perception
- Hidden motives
- Self-awareness
- Pattern recognition
- Influence without force
- Reputation management
- Energy and boundaries
- Strategic patience
